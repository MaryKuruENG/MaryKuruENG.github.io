title: "Smart Fuel Level Detection and Alarm System for Gensets" excerpt: "Automated IoT-based monitoring system designed for industrial generator sets to optimize fuel management and prevent operational bottlenecks during heavy equipment servicing." collection: portfolio --- ### Project Overview This project addressed industrial monitoring challenges by developing an automated fuel level detection and alert system tailored for heavy equipment generator sets (gensets). It eliminates manual dipstick checks and provides real-time visibility over fluid levels. ### Key Contributions * **Hardware & Prototyping:** Integrated ultrasonic and float-based fluid level sensors with microcontroller hardware (Arduino/ESP32) for continuous data acquisition. * **Automated Alert System:** Configured automated buzzer and LED warning indicators for low-level thresholds to prevent unexpected genset shutdowns on-site. * **Industrial Application:** Designed to streamline maintenance workflows and reduce equipment downtime in heavy-duty machinery environments.
