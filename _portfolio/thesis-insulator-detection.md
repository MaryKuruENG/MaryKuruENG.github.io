### Thesis Overview
This research evaluated the automated detection and classification of high-voltage transmission line insulator defects using computer vision and deep learning techniques. 

### Key Contributions
* Developed and trained a **VGGNet-16 deep learning model** in Python to classify insulator conditions as defective or healthy.
* Implemented an image-based predictive maintenance pipeline to catch structural faults early and minimize electrical utility grid downtime.
* Achieved **98% training accuracy**, proving the viability of automated computer vision for remote electrical infrastructure monitoring.

### Technologies
Python, TensorFlow, Keras, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, VGGNet-16
