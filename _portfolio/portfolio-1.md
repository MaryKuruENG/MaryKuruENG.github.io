### Overview
Developed and trained a VGGNet-16 deep learning model to automatically analyze, detect failures, and classify high-voltage transmission line insulators as defective or healthy.

### Technologies Used
* Python, TensorFlow, Keras, Computer Vision, Deep Learning

### Impact
Achieved a 98% training accuracy, implementing an image-based predictive maintenance pipeline to catch structural faults early and minimize utility grid downtime.
