I am a Mechatronics Engineering master's student at Ashesi University working toward building autonomous, intelligent machines for Ghana's mining industry by embedding AI directly into hardware.

* **Education:** MSc./MAS Mechatronics Engineering at Ashesi University (expected Sep 2028); BSc Electrical and Electronic Engineering, University of Mines and Technology (2023).
* **Industry experience:** Electrical Trainee at Sandvik Mining and Construction (2023 to 2025), working on rock drill rebuilds and heavy equipment harness routing.
* **Research and interests:** Deep learning for predictive maintenance, embedded systems, control systems and autonomous machines that reduce human risk in hazardous environments.
* **Leadership:** Chair of the IEEE Student Branch at Ashesi, Program Lead for EngineerHER, and an invited speaker for IEEE Communications Society, UET Taxila.
