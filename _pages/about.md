## Leadership and community

I chair the **IEEE Student Branch at Ashesi University**, led the **EngineerHER** outreach program for 30 senior high school girls, and was an invited speaker for the **IEEE Communications Society, UET Taxila**, speaking on designing autonomous systems that eliminate human risk in hazardous environments.

I am passionate about responsible AI adoption and about building a community where people learn, build with and use AI tools well.
